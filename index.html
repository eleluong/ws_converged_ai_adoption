About ConnectED & Agentic AI Stack

ConnectED & The Emerging Agentic AI Stack represents a research and workshop syllabus mapping specialized local domain expertise (Vietnamese education curriculum) into state-of-the-art agent frameworks.

This UI parses split markdown documents directly from the ref_doc folder of the workspace, organizing content into topics:

  • 01. Platform: Focuses on the ADDIE instruction framework, localization, and impact.
  • 02. Stack: Explores orchestration, reasoning, skills, MCP protocols, and commerce trust mandates.
  • 03. Challenges: Addresses latency, costs, trajectory evaluation, and AgentOps.
  • 04. Checklists: Interactive checklists to prepare your agentic deployment.
